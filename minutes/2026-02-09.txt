MEETING OF THE BOARD
VIRTUAL VIA ZOOM
MONDAY, FEBRUARY 9, 2026
A regular meeting of the Board of the St. Louis County Library District was held virtually via Zoom on Monday, February 9, 2026, at 4:00 p.m. The agenda had been posted in all branches of the Library District at least 24 hours before the meeting.

NEW BUSINESS
Construction Contract for the Guy and Judy Jaffe Family Branch
Roberto Munster, CAO, presented the recommendation.
Library Administration recommends the selection of ICS Construction Services, Ltd. as providing the lowest and best general contractor solution for construction of the new Guy and Judy Jaffe Family Branch at a cost of $11,277,000 and that the Board authorize the Director and CEO to enter into and execute a contract to that effect.
Motion by Lynn Beckwith, second by Ted Sanditz.
Final Resolution: Motion Carries
Aye: Johnny Wang, Deborah Patterson, Ted Sanditz, Lynn Beckwith, May Scheve Reardon
